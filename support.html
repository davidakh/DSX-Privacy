Frequently asked questions

What controllers does DSX support?

DSX is built for Sony DualSense and DualSense Edge controllers, connected over USB or Bluetooth.

Do I need a controller connected to use DSX?

You can explore the full interface without one, but a compatible controller must be connected to apply settings and use haptics, rumble, and audio features.

How do the audio and haptics features work?

DSX can route system audio to your controller's speaker, audio jack, rumble, or haptics. This is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded or transmitted. You can enable or disable these features at any time.

How do I share or back up my profiles?

DSX lets you export controller profiles as files and import them again later, so you can back them up or share them with others.

What is DSX+?

DSX+ is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks additional features. Purchases are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.

Something isn't working — what should I do?

Join the Discord or email us with your Mac model, macOS version, controller type, and a description of the issue. We'll help you sort it out.
