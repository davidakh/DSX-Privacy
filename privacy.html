DSX Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

DSX is a companion utility for Sony DualSense controllers, developed by Paliverse Apps. This policy explains how DSX handles your information. In short: DSX does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

DSX does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to any third party.

Information we collect

None. DSX operates entirely on your device. It does not gather personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers, and it does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Audio processing

DSX includes optional features that use system audio to drive your controller's speaker, audio jack, rumble, and haptics. This audio is processed only on your device and in real time. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. These features are optional and can be enabled or disabled by you at any time.

Controller data

DSX communicates with connected controllers over USB and Bluetooth solely to read input and apply your settings. This data stays on your device and is not collected or shared.

Profile files

DSX lets you import and export controller profiles as files you choose. These files remain under your control on your own device or wherever you decide to save or share them. Paliverse Apps has no access to them.

Purchases

DSX offers an optional in-app purchase (DSX+). All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Paliverse Apps does not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Third parties

DSX does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Children's privacy

DSX does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Paliverse Apps at support@paliverseapps.com.
